The route

Islands and Bosses

Every island has a quest NPC. From the second island on there are two enemy groups: the second gives more XP and only unlocks at a certain level. After that comes the boss quest.

    The powers

    All Fruits

    Every fruit has four attacks on Z, X, C and V. You unlock new attacks with mastery, which you earn by fighting with the fruit. Mastery is saved separately for each fruit.

    Legendary: +15% damage Mythical: +35% damage Fruit stat: +1% fruit damage per point
    Fruit dealer Fio

    Try your luck

    You pull fruits from the fruit dealer Fio with Splitz, once every 2 hours. Most of the time you get nothing, sometimes a fruit. Here you can pull as often as you like, with the real odds.

    1. Pull. A fruit you pull goes into your hand or inventory.
    2. Equip. Tap it and you hold it in your hand.
    3. Eat. The powers are yours right away. An eaten fruit never comes back.
    4. Gain mastery. Fight with the fruit to unlock X, C and V.
    Pulls: 0 Fruits: 0 Best find: –

    Just for fun. In the game every pull costs Splitz and only works every 2 hours.

    Mythical forms

    Transformations

    Jaguar and Scharingan each have a transformation that you charge with its own meter.

    Jaguar fruit

    Jaguar Form

    You become a glowing blue jaguar: faster, able to run on water, fighting with Bite (launch and slam), Claw Whirl and Jaguar Leap. Your hits poison.

    Scharingan fruit

    Blutwächter

    A 110-stud red giant with arm strikes, a blood disc and a stomp. With T it creates a giant sword, grabs enemies and slams them into the ground.

    1. Deal damage with the fruit. The more damage, the faster the meter fills.
    2. When it's full, “METER VOLL” appears.
    3. Press V to transform. The meter drains slowly and each attack takes a bit more.
    4. During a full moon the transformation lasts 30 seconds longer.
    Combat and PvP

    How to win fights

    • Auto-aim. Attacks lock onto the nearest target. Even a near miss still hits.
    • Build combos. Many attacks pull enemies in (Dreck Z, Frost Z, Schatten Z, Flamme C) or knock them away (Jaguar Bite, Erdhügel, Stoß-Kugel).
    • Air combo. Enemies in the air or in a grab take 20% more damage.
    • Block. Hold R to reduce damage.
    • Enemy AI. Enemies only attack when you stand right in front of them. Take more than 25% of their health and they get angry and chase you until you leave their area.

    Controls

    PC and Mobile

    On PC you play with mouse and keyboard, on mobile with round buttons on the right. Under Settings → “Anzeigen verschieben” you can move, resize and lock your HUD and buttons.

    Home and bounty

    The Home NPC sets your return point and the house button takes you there (not during combat). Your bounty only rises when you defeat a player of the other side.

    Boats

    From boatbuilder Kalle. Boats you buy are yours forever and can be called at any dock. Steer with W.

    The world

    Day, Night and Moon

    Open sea lies between the islands. Swim on the surface or take a boat. At night the lanterns light up.
